Our Story

A Welcome That Feels Like Coming Home

Grand Luxe Hotel began with a simple belief: the most memorable journeys are shaped by the people who welcome you. What started as a small hospitality vision has grown into a place where thoughtful details and warm service make every stay feel special.

Today, our team brings that original spirit to every guest, pairing the comfort of a private retreat with the care and confidence of world-class hospitality.

Our mission is to make each visit effortless, personal, and worth returning for—whether you are here to explore Kigali, celebrate an occasion, or simply pause and unwind.
